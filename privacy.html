Privacy Policy

Candle: Chart Puzzle Game · Last updated September 29, 2026

Candle is a puzzle game made by Mohammad Al Rebh ("I", "me"). This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: there are no accounts, I run no servers that store your data, and your game progress stays on your phone. The only data that leaves your device is what the ads need, described below.

What stays on your device

Your streaks, win and loss stats, puzzle progress, chosen theme, reminder settings and Pro status are stored locally on your iPhone. I can't see them, and I can't recover them if you delete the app.

Ads

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • A device identifier, such as the advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking) or an identifier limited to this app
  • Advertising data, such as which ads were shown, and how you interacted with them
  • Performance and crash data, such as app launch time or crash logs

This data goes to Google and its advertising partners, not to me. Google's own policies apply to it: policies.google.com/privacy.

If you buy Pro, the app shows no ads at all.

Your choices about ads

  • Tracking. On iOS, the app asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no, ads can still appear, but they will not be personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app shows a consent form before ads load. If you're in one of those regions, a "Privacy options" row appears in the app's Settings so you can change your choices later.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. I never see your payment details, name or Apple Account. The app only checks with the App Store whether Pro has been unlocked, and "Restore Purchase" uses the same check.

Checking today's date

The daily puzzle depends on the correct date, so the app makes a simple request to a public server (Apple or Google) to read the current time. Like any internet request, this reveals your IP address to that server. I don't send or store anything about you in this request.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server. You can turn it off in the app or in iOS Settings.

What I don't do

  • I don't create accounts or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • I don't use analytics or crash-reporting tools of my own.
  • I don't sell your data.

Children

Candle is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Not financial advice

Candle is a game for fun and learning. The charts are real market data, but nothing in the app is investment advice.

Your rights

Because I don't hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for me to access or delete. Data held by Google for ads is governed by Google's tools and policies. If you'd like to ask me anything about this policy, or make a request under privacy law where you live, write to me and I'll reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
