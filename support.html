Candle · Privacy Policy

Support

Something not working, or a question about Candle? Email mhalrebh@atomicmail.io. I read every message and usually reply within a few days. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug.

I bought Pro but it's locked

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase, using the same Apple Account you bought with. If that doesn't work, email me.

My streak or progress disappeared

Progress is stored only on your phone. Deleting the app, or resetting the phone without restoring from a backup, removes it. A lost streak can't be recovered from my side, but write to me and I'll look into it.

The daily puzzle says "Connect to the internet to play"

The app checks today's date online so the daily puzzle can't be changed by editing the phone's clock. Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data and try again.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through them: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy

See the Privacy Policy. You can change your ad choices in the app's Settings, or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
